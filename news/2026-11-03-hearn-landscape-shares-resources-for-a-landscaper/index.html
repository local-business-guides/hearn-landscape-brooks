BROOKS, Georgia (November 3, 2026) Hearn Landscape Management has released a guide for homeowners searching for a landscaper near me, along with a related video, podcast episode and audio version.

The guide offers practical points for people deciding what kind of landscaping help they need and what to ask a company before starting a project. Homeowners can use it to think about their yard's condition, the work they want done and which services might fit their plans. It also gives readers a starting point for discussing project details and understanding what to expect from an estimate.

That preparation can help people make better use of an initial conversation with a landscaper. Hearn Landscape Management handles projects ranging from sod installation and landscape design to grading, drainage, pavers, patios, irrigation and outdoor lighting. Knowing which concerns matter most to you can make it easier to explain your goals and ask about the work involved.

The guide is accompanied by a video on YouTube, a podcast episode and an audio version on SoundCloud. Homeowners in Brooks and nearby communities can use the materials as they plan an outdoor project and consider which questions to bring to an estimate. Hearn Landscape Management says customers work with the same team member from the first estimate through the finished project, with open communication along the way. Homeowners can schedule an estimate to discuss their needs.

About Hearn Landscape

Hearn Landscape Management is a family-owned landscaping company based in Brooks, Georgia, serving homeowners in Griffin, Fayetteville, Hampton, Zebulon, McDonough and nearby communities. Brothers Sam and Daniel Hearn started the business in 2008, and its team handles landscaping, outdoor living, irrigation, concrete and land clearing projects.
