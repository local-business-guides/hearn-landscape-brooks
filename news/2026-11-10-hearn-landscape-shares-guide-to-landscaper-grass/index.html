BROOKS, Georgia (November 10, 2026) Hearn Landscape has published a guide about landscaper grass in Brooks, along with a YouTube video, a podcast episode and an audio version on SoundCloud. The materials are for homeowners considering grass work and sod installation around Brooks and nearby communities.

The guide covers practical points to think about before starting a grass project, including yard preparation and care after sod is installed. Those details can help homeowners plan what needs to happen before the work begins and understand that care continues after installation. The information is useful for people comparing their options or preparing to request an estimate.

Hearn Landscape says its sod installation work includes grading and preparing the yard before laying sod. The company also shares care tips for the months ahead. Reviewing those topics can give homeowners a clearer starting point for discussing their yard and project needs with a landscaper.

Readers can find the guide through Hearn Landscape. The companion video is on YouTube, and the audio version is on SoundCloud. A podcast episode is also available. Homeowners who want to discuss a project can schedule an estimate with the company.

About Hearn Landscape

Hearn Landscape Management is a family-owned landscaping company based in Brooks, Georgia, serving homeowners in Griffin, Fayetteville, Hampton, Zebulon, McDonough and nearby communities. Brothers Sam and Daniel Hearn started the business in 2008. The team handles sod installation, landscape design, retaining walls, grading and drainage, pavers, patios and fire pits, irrigation, outdoor lighting, concrete, land clearing and brush removal.
