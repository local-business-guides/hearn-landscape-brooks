BROOKS, Georgia (October 27, 2026) Hearn Landscape has released a guide about local landscaping in Brooks, along with a related YouTube video and podcast episode.

The materials are intended for homeowners considering landscaping work in Brooks and nearby communities. They offer a starting point for learning about local landscaping services and thinking through what to discuss when planning a yard project. The guide focuses on the topic of finding a local landscaper in Brooks.

Hearn Landscape handles projects including sod installation, landscape design, retaining walls, grading and drainage, pavers, patios and fire pits, irrigation installation and repair, outdoor lighting, concrete, land clearing and brush removal. For homeowners considering new sod, the company grades and prepares the yard before installation and shares care tips for the months ahead. These are among the practical details residents may want to consider when weighing the work a property needs.

The resource may be useful to homeowners at the early planning stage, before they arrange an estimate. Hearn Landscape says customers work with the same team member from the first estimate through the finished project, with open communication along the way. The company invites homeowners to schedule an estimate to get started.

The related video is on YouTube. The business information provided for this release did not include a direct link to the guide or a platform for the podcast episode. Homeowners can contact Hearn Landscape to schedule an estimate, though no phone number was provided.

## About Hearn Landscape
Hearn Landscape Management is a family-owned landscaping company based in Brooks, Georgia, serving homeowners in Griffin, Fayetteville, Hampton, Zebulon, McDonough and nearby communities. Brothers Sam and Daniel Hearn started the business in 2008. The company handles landscaping and outdoor projects, including sod, design, drainage, pavers, irrigation and land clearing.
