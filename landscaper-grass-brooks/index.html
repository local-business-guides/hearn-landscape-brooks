## A short checklist before sod work
Before scheduling an estimate, take a walk around the area and gather a few notes:

  • Mark the sections you want covered and any areas you want left open.
  • Point out spots where water gathers or the ground seems uneven.
  • Mention recent or planned work nearby, such as a patio, path or irrigation system.
  • Consider how people and pets use the lawn.
  • Ask what preparation and aftercare you should plan for.

These details help set clear expectations. They can also bring up issues that should be discussed before sod is laid, rather than after the lawn is in place. If grading or drainage needs attention, mention it when talking over the project.

## Avoid rushing past the ground preparation
One common mistake is focusing only on the visible grass and overlooking the condition of the area underneath it. Uneven ground and drainage concerns can affect the finished space, so they deserve attention as part of the plan.
